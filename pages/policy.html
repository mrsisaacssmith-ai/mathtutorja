Cancellation & lateness policy

Please read this before registering. Parents and students confirm agreement at sign-up.

24h

Cancellation notice

Classes must be cancelled or rescheduled at least 24 hours before the session start time. Cancellations made after this window, or missed sessions without notice, are counted as the week's class and are not refunded or made up.

⏱

Lateness

Sessions run for their full scheduled length regardless of what time the student joins. If a student arrives late — online or face to face — the session still ends at the scheduled time, and no extra time is added.

📅

Monthly payment

Payment is due at the start of each month to keep a weekly slot reserved. If payment is not received, the slot may be released to another student.
